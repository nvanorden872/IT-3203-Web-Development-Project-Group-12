Search Engine Optimization

Introduction

This site is a project site for IT 3203 Web Development and explores the concept and applications of SEO (Search Engine Optimization). Topics include the history of SEO, its current uses and debates, and an explanation of how it works and why it is important.

Key Terms
SEO Concept Meaning
Keywords Words people type in search engines to find your page.
Title Tag The page title that appears in search results and browser tabs.
Meta Description A short summary that appears under your title in search results.
Alt Text Describes images so search engines and screen readers understand them.
Links Connections between pages that help users and search engines navigate.
Mobile Friendly Making sure your website looks good and works well on phones.
Page Speed How fast your website loads for visitors.
Content Quality Having useful, clear, and original information on your page.
